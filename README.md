# Wellspring Pharmacy Workbench Demo

An interactive sales demo of the pharmacy side of the Wellspring portal. You play Laura Whitfield, PharmD, at **Larkspur Compounding Pharmacy**, a fictitious non-sterile 503A pharmacy with 9 prescribers across 5 practices.

Open `index.html` in a browser (or enable GitHub Pages for this repo) and click **Enter the demo as Laura**.

## What it shows

- **Orders to fill** – Awaiting payment, Inbound, In Fulfillment and Shipped, with the 5-step progress bar
- **Paid vs. unpaid** – patient-pay orders stay locked until the patient pays
- **Complete prescriptions** – the signed prescription as received, compliance checks and an audit trail
- **Clarifications and unable to fill** – handled inside the order
- **Add Rx # / RPh review**, then automated FedEx labels and tracking
- **Practices** – practice groups, prescriber credentials, compliance blocks and onboarding
- **Catalogs, Billing & Payouts, Order history, Patients, Messages and Learn**
- **Demo guide** – 9 guided tasks, each showing what changes for the pharmacy with Wellspring

All pharmacy, practice, prescriber and patient data is fictitious. Nothing is sent, charged or stored.
